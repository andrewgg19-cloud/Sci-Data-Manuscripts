# Ethos

German Gamma is shared in the spirit of collective knowledge building.

The project invites:

```text
scrutiny
replication
correction
responsible reuse
collective improvement
```

Its claims must remain bounded by:

```text
evidence
validation
explicit interpretation limits
domain expertise
reproducible tests
```

## Spanish Note

German Gamma se comparte con gratitud hacia la construccion colectiva del
conocimiento.

El proyecto invita a la revision, replicacion, correccion y reutilizacion
responsable.

Sus afirmaciones deben mantenerse dentro de los limites de la evidencia, la
validacion y la interpretacion explicita.

## Final Principle

```text
Knowledge becomes stronger when it is shared, tested, corrected, and improved
collectively.
```
