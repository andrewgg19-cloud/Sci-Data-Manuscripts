My name is Andrés Germán García, an independent researcher in Mexico. Your work with Cheng Tao Yang on cosmological strangeness abundance is a reference in two related manuscripts I have deposited as preprints on Zenodo.

The first reconstructs equilibrium baryon and antibaryon populations at 50 MeV using fixed hadron-resonance-gas inventories and a prescribed net-baryon-to-entropy ratio. It reproduces the approximately 38.2 MeV crossing and makes explicit why the net excess alone does not identify a unique pair population or history:
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23467572

The companion study examines entropy injection and effective pair kinetics between 50 and 30 MeV under a hypothetical black-hole evaporation model. It distinguishes common-initial-yield and common-final-yield comparisons. It does not infer the origin of the baryon asymmetry or establish the assumed black-hole population observationally:
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23467637
